Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IMSIYA (“we”, “our” or “the marketplace”) may collect, use and share information when people use the IMSIYA website, app and marketplace services.

Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email address and contact information.
  • Seller profile information, shop details, product listings, descriptions and images.
  • Order details, delivery information, customer messages and transaction records.
  • Technical information such as device, app usage and diagnostic data needed to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

We use information to create and manage accounts, display shops and products, process orders, coordinate customer and seller activity, provide support, prevent fraud, maintain records and improve the marketplace.

Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers, including PayPal where offered. Payment providers process payment credentials under their own privacy policies and terms. IMSIYA does not ask users to send passwords or payment-card details through ordinary messages.

When information may be shared

Information may be shared with the relevant customer or seller to complete an order, with payment and technical service providers needed to operate the marketplace, or where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal and accounting obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. We use appropriate safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access to or correction of account information, or ask about account closure, through the support channel made available in the IMSIYA app or service. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Children

IMSIYA is not intended for children under 18 to create seller accounts or make purchases without the involvement and permission of a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy as the marketplace develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

Questions about this policy can be raised through the support channel provided in the IMSIYA app or marketplace.
