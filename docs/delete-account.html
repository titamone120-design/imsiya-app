Delete your IMSIYA account

You can request deletion of your IMSIYA account and personal profile from inside the app or website.

How to submit a request

  1. Open IMSIYA and sign in to the account you want deleted.
  2. Open My Account / Profile.
  3. Select Request account deletion.
  4. Confirm and submit the request.
Open IMSIYA

What happens next

An IMSIYA administrator reviews the request and processes deletion. The account and personal profile are deleted after processing. Administrator accounts cannot be deleted through customer deletion requests.

Records that may be retained

Order, payment, payout, tax, dispute and security records may be retained where required by law or needed for accounting, fraud prevention or resolving disputes. Account identifiers are removed from retained marketplace records where possible.

Seller account deletion may be delayed until available wallet balances, pending payouts and paid orders still in progress have been resolved. Product listings are disabled and product images are removed when a seller account is deleted.

Your account remains active while a request is awaiting review. Submitting a request does not immediately sign you out or delete the account.
