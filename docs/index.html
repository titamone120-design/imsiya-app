How IMSIYA works

Made for customers and sellers.

01 · Sellers join

Independent sellers can create an account and set up a shop, subject to platform requirements.

02 · Customers discover

Customers can browse available products and place orders through the marketplace.

03 · Orders and payments

Customers pay using the payment method displayed at checkout. Sellers keep 100% of verified product sales; IMSIYA does not charge a sales commission. Seller shop access costs USD $20 per month, after seller approval and verified subscription payment. Payout requests are reviewed by IMSIYA and marked paid only after the transfer has been made and confirmed.

Our purpose

Helping small sweet businesses reach more customers.

IMSIYA aims to give independent sweet-food sellers an online place to present their products and connect with customers. Product availability, delivery arrangements and seller participation may vary.
